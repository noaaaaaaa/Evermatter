Why electrolyte recovery matters

Battery recycling should not end at black mass.

The water stream still carries electrolyte-derived material. We are developing a recovery route that begins with selective adsorption, rather than treating that value only as a disposal problem.

Selective capture

Alternating duties

Product recovery
